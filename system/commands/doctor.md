# Command: /doctor

Audit knowledge-base structure, portfolio health, registries, sync state, templates, adapters, daily surfaces, and links.

- `/doctor`: read-only audit.
- `/doctor fix`: apply clear safe repairs and preview destructive or ambiguous changes.

Follow `system/procedures/doctor.md`. In read-only mode, do not log, commit, or push. In fix mode, validate again and follow repository sync policy.
