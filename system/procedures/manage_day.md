# Procedure: Manage The Day

## `/day start`

Regenerate `wiki/today.md`; do not append to the previous brief.

1. Resolve the date and read registries, operational status, pending queues, compact logs, portfolio state, relevant Context Cards, and inbox captures.
2. Write date/generated metadata plus `Now`, `Next`, `Waiting`, `Inbox`, and `System attention`.
3. Keep `Now` to at most three concrete actions.
4. Include only pending ingestion or sync failures under system attention.
5. Do not create a human log merely for regenerating the view.

## `/day close`

1. Follow `system/procedures/organise_scratch.md` for the knowledge-base inbox.
2. Read today's brief and meaningful updates.
3. Finalize the day only from meaningful work, not routine sync telemetry.
4. Promote durable outcomes before closing.
5. Carry unfinished work deliberately: blocked to `waiting`, intentionally inactive to `parked`, ready follow-ups to `next`.
6. Regenerate the brief as a closed-day view or prepare the next date when explicitly requested.
7. Update log indexes and summaries through `maintain_daily_log.md`.

Keep external project repositories unchanged unless the user explicitly authorizes a named project-memory update.
